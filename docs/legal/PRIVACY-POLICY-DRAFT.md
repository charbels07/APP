# Privacy policy — Empower Communication

Version `[number]`, `[date]`.

## 1. Who we are

Empower Communication is an app that helps children who cannot rely on speech to communicate
with pictures and a voice (augmentative and alternative communication, "AAC"). It is published by
`[Company name]`, `[legal form]`, `[address]`, `[registration number]` ("we").

- Questions about your data and requests: `[privacy e-mail]`
- Our data protection officer: `[DPO]`, `[DPO e-mail]`

We are the **controller** of the data described here: we decide why and how it is used.

## 2. In short

- The child's board is stored on your device and works without internet. Our server keeps a copy
  so that it reaches the child's other devices and the people you invite.
- **You decide** what the app records about how your child communicates. It is off by default.
- We never sell data, never show advertising, never use your data to profile you for marketing.
- The child's sentences are only kept if you choose the level "Use, words and sentences".
- Our team only sees anonymous figures, only if you agree, and never for fewer than 5 children.
- You can download all your data and delete your account in the app at any time.

## 3. Whose data

| Person | How they use the app |
|---|---|
| **Parents** (the family) | Create the account and the child's profile; choose who joins the child's team and what is recorded |
| **The child** | Uses the board. The child has no account and no e-mail address; their profile belongs to the parent's account |
| **Professionals** (speech therapists, teachers…) | Have their own account; work on a child's board only when the family invites them or accepts their request |
| **Other adults of the team** (family members, carers) | Invited by the parents, with the rights the parents give |

| Your account, the child's profile, the team, the follow-up | Our server provider, Supabase, in the European Union (`[Frankfurt or Paris]`) |

shared computer you can also erase the photos and recordings.

## 6. Children's data, parental consent and health-related data

- **The parent decides.** Only the parent who owns the child's profile (or a person they allow
  to manage the team) chooses what is recorded and who sees it. The agreement is recorded with
  its date and the version of the text.
- **Health-related data.** That a child uses an AAC app, how they communicate, and their
  follow-up can reveal a disability. We treat this as a special category of data (GDPR art. 9),
  used only with the parent's explicit consent and only to provide the service.
  *To confirm: whether explicit consent is the right basis for the board itself, or only for the
  follow-up and observations; the wording of the consent screen.*
- **A profile prepared by a professional.** A verified professional can prepare a child's
  profile before the family has an account, then hand it to the parents. Until then, recording
  and sharing stay off. The parents then choose everything themselves.
- **Age.** The child never has an account. *To confirm: the age of digital consent in each
  country, and what changes when the child grows up (for example, informing an older child).*

## 9. Voices and dictation

- **The voice that speaks** is your device's own voice, or a recording made by your family. We
  use no paid speech service.
- **Dictation** (saying a word to search it, dictating a sentence to show it on the board) uses
  your device's speech recognition (Apple or Google). Where the system offers it, recognition
  happens on the device. Otherwise **your device may send the audio to Apple or Google** to turn
  it into text, under their own privacy policies. We do not receive or keep the audio.

## 11. How we protect data

- The server checks every request: a person only sees the children whose team they are on, and
  only what the family allows. Someone who is not signed in can read nothing.
- Files are in private folders. Identity documents are deleted after the check.
- Passwords are stored scrambled. Two-step sign-in is required for our team and offered to
  parents. On phones and tablets, the session is kept in the device's secure storage (iOS
  Keychain, Android Keystore).
- Deleting an account or a child's profile, linking a full-access device, or downloading all
  data asks for a fresh proof (your password, or a code sent to your e-mail).
- Our team's figures need at least 5 children of 5 families; our team never sees a name, a word
  or a sentence of a child.
- Backups are encrypted when kept outside our server provider.
