# Terms of use — Daleth AAC

Version `[number]`, `[date]`. Published by DALETH, Beirut, Lebanon ("we").

## 1. What the app is

Daleth AAC is an augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) app: a board of
pictures and words that speaks for a child who cannot rely on speech. It works on iPhone, iPad
and Android, in French, English and Arabic, and is sold in the App Store and Google Play only. A
web version is open today to chosen testers; it is not offered to families. *(Reviewer: whether
testers of the web version need terms of their own.)* Families use it with the child; professionals
(speech therapists, teachers…) can join a child's team when the family allows it.

## 2. Two commitments we make

1. **The child will always be able to communicate.** The child's board is stored on the device
   and works without internet. Whatever happens to a subscription, to our server or to us, we
   will not switch the board off on the child's device. *(Billing is not built yet: the exact
   effect of an unpaid subscription on the other features — sync, team, follow-up — is to write
   with it. The continuity pledge says what happens if the service stops:
   `CONTINUITY-PLEDGE-DRAFT.md`.)*
2. **The positions of the words do not move** by themselves. The place of each core word is the
   child's motor plan: updates of the app never move it; only an adult of the family can.

## 3. What the app is not

- **It is not a medical device and not an assessment.** It does not replace an assessment by a
  speech and language therapist or another qualified professional. The follow-up figures and
  the "communicative functions" are guesses from simple rules, to help the team talk; they are
  not a diagnosis.
- **Keep another way to communicate.** A device can break, run out of battery, be lost or be
  stolen. Keep a backup way for the child to communicate: a printed board (the app prints its
  boards: "Print / PDF"), another device, gestures the people around the child know.
- The pictograms chosen automatically must be checked by an adult before the child uses them.
  *(The AI helper is switched off today; if it is offered one day, its suggestions too.)*
- The vocabulary in Arabic dialects and the words added for some picture libraries are first
  versions marked "to review" where the app says so.

## 4. Accounts

- An account is for one person. Do not share your password. Turn on two-step sign-in.
- **Families.** The parent who creates a child's profile is responsible for it: they choose who
  joins the team, the rights of each person, and what is recorded. They confirm that they have
  parental authority over the child, or the agreement of the person who has it.
  *(To confirm: the case of separated parents, and of an adult user without a parent.)*
- **Professionals.** A professional account must be verified (diploma or licence, identity)
  before it can join a child's team or create a child's profile. The professional:
  - uses only the rights the family gives, and only for the child's care or education;
  - keeps what she sees confidential under the rules of her profession;
  - does not record, export or share the child's data outside the app without the family's
    agreement;
  - when she prepares a profile for a family, hands it over with the parents' code only, to the
    parents only; recording and sharing stay off until then;
  - uses her own caregiver code on the boards she opens, never the family's.
- **A tablet linked with a code** acts only on the child's profile it was linked to. A family
  removes it in Settings → Account → Devices.
- **Our team's accounts** are never used on a device a child uses.

## 5. The caregiver code and locking the screen

The caregiver code keeps the adults' tools away from the child's hands. It is not a password:
choose one that the child does not know. The app cannot keep a child inside it by itself: to do
that, use the device's own feature (Guided Access on iPhone and iPad, app pinning on Android).
"Lock the screen" in the app explains how and opens the device's settings.

## 6. Your content

- What you add (words, photos, recordings, boards, notes) stays yours. You give us the right to
  store it, copy it between your devices and show it to the people you invite, only to provide
  the service.
- Add only photos and recordings you have the right to use. Do not write a child's name or
  medical information in messages to our team.
- The pictograms come from libraries whose licences allow this use (credits: Settings → Board →
  About). Their licences stay with their authors.

## 7. Acceptable use

Do not: try to reach data you were not given; test the security without our written agreement;
use the app to harm a child or anyone else; copy or resell the app; overload the service; use
another person's account. We may suspend an account that does, after a warning when the risk
allows it. Suspending an account never switches off the board already on a child's device.

- If we stop the service, we follow the continuity pledge (notice, exports).
