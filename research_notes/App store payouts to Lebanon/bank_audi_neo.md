## 1. What is "neo" at Bank Audi? (product name, launch, onboarding, currencies, "fresh" status)

### Takeaway
"Bank Audi Neo" is real. Its official name is **"neo by Bank Audi"** (lower-case "neo", website neo.com.lb). Bank Audi presents it as **Lebanon's first digital bank**, launched in **2024** and built on Codebase Technologies' "Digibanc" platform. It is opened in the app with a phone and a national ID or passport. It holds **Fresh USD and Fresh LBP accounts**, with other currencies listed too, and comes with a physical and a digital debit card.

There is also a separate **"neo Business"** app for businesses. In **September 2026**, Bank Audi and Mastercard launched a programme offering 10,000 small businesses free neo Business accounts and Mastercard business debit cards.

### Inferences
- **The user's "Audi Neo" is almost certainly "neo by Bank Audi"**, the retail digital bank. "neo Business" is the business variant. A neo account opened in 2024 or later can only hold post-2019 money, so it is a fresh account by construction. The listing's own "Fresh USD account" wording fits this.
- **Two different Bank Audi routes exist for an Audi customer:**
  1. a **neo Fresh USD account** (app-only, cheap monthly fee, app limits on incoming funds; see Q2);
  2. a classic Bank Audi **External Account** (branch-based, Circular 150 fresh account, published tiered incoming-wire fees).
- If the user already has a classic Bank Audi account, they should check whether it is an External (fresh) account or a legacy ("lollar") account. Payouts must never land in a legacy account.
- **Personal or business account:** Apple and Google pay an individual developer in that individual's name, so a personal neo or External account in the developer's own name matches the store records. Nothing found says Bank Audi *requires* a business account to receive app-store income. "neo Business" / "Business External Account" would mainly matter if the developer sets up a company. That would also change the Apple and Google contracting entity and tax forms.

## 2. Can neo / Bank Audi fresh USD accounts receive international SWIFT wires from Apple and Google? (BIC, IBAN, fees, correspondent, delays, limits, compliance)

### Takeaway
**Yes, in principle.** neo's own materials list receiving international transfers by IBAN into a neo account. Bank Audi publishes incoming-wire fees for fresh External Accounts. Bank Audi's BIC is **AUDBLBBX** (AUDBLBBXXXX in 11-character form).

The weak points are:
- neo's **daily and monthly limits on incoming funds**, with amounts not published in what I could see;
- the fact that **no source names Bank Audi's USD correspondent bank**;
- likely source-of-funds questions for recurring foreign business income.

Fees seen:
- **neo:** incoming IBAN transfers appear as **free** in the neo brochure. Outgoing transfers cost **USD 25 / LBP 350,000**.
- **Bank Audi External Account:** incoming transfers are tiered at **USD 5 (≤ 500), 10 (≤ 3,000), 15 (≤ 10,000), 20 (> 10,000)**.

### Inferences
- **Costs per payout:**
  - Into a **neo** account: likely **USD 0** in neo fees, if the "Free" reading is right. Intermediary or correspondent deductions may still come off the wire before it arrives; the sender's and intermediary banks' charges are outside Bank Audi's tariff.
  - Into an **External Account**: **USD 5–20 per payout**. On a USD 1,000 monthly payout that is USD 10, about 1%. A USD 5 fee on small early payouts (≤ USD 500) is proportionally heavy. Apple pays only once the minimum threshold is met, and Google pays monthly, so small months can be expensive.
- **neo limits are the main practical risk.** If neo's monthly incoming-funds cap is lower than a payout, an Apple or Google wire could be returned or held. Ask the bank in writing for the incoming-wire limit on a neo Fresh USD account, and whether it can be raised for documented business income. If not, use a classic **External Account** for payouts and neo for day-to-day spending.
- **Details to put in App Store Connect / Play Console:**
  - account holder name exactly as on the account;
  - the holder's Lebanese address, matching the bank's records (Apple requirement; see earlier notes);
  - bank name "Bank Audi S.A.L.", BIC **AUDBLBBX** or **AUDBLBBXXXX**;
  - the account's **IBAN** (shown in the neo app).
  - Use the branch details the bank gives, not an intermediary bank, unless Bank Audi explicitly asks for one. An Apple forum case in the earlier notes points the same way.
- **IBAN format:** to my knowledge, a Lebanese IBAN is **28 characters in total**: "LB", 2 check digits, a 4-digit bank code and a 20-character account number. That is *not* "LB + 28". This comes from my background knowledge of the SWIFT IBAN registry and was not verified in this session.
- **Compliance likely to come up (inference from Lebanese bank practice and the FATF/EU status in the earlier notes; no Bank-Audi-specific document list was found):**
  - a declaration of the source of income or occupation ("software developer / app publisher") on the KYC profile;
  - possibly a copy of the Apple Developer Program License Agreement / Paid Apps Agreement or the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement;
  - payout statements (App Store Connect "Payments and Financial Reports", Play Console earnings reports);
  - for large or unusual amounts, an explanation of the activity.
  - Lebanon's 2025 secrecy-law changes and grey-listing make such requests more likely, not less.
- Apple and Google send **regular, same-origin wires** (same remitter and reference format). After the first one or two are cleared with documents, later ones usually pass more easily. This is a general banking pattern, not a sourced Bank Audi fact.

### Gaps
- Exact neo incoming-funds limits (daily and monthly, in USD) were not seen.
- Whether incoming SWIFT wires into **neo** are really free, or carry the External Account's USD 5–20 tariff or a per-mille charge, was not confirmed.
- **Bank Audi's USD correspondent / intermediary bank** (likely a large US or European bank) was not found. Value dates (working days) for incoming wires from abroad were not read.

- Not confirmed: whether Google lets a Lebanese payments profile be paid in USD rather than LBP. If Google pays in LBP, the wire would arrive in LBP or be converted, which matters for a Fresh USD account. **This is the biggest open question for Google.**

## 3. Fresh-money rules at Bank Audi / neo: cash withdrawal, transfers abroad, fees, 2025–2026 changes

### Takeaway
Fresh USD at Bank Audi can be withdrawn as USD cash:
- **neo:** free at Bank Audi ATMs using an in-app "Cash Out" voucher, no card needed (per the Feb 2024 brochure); about **6‰ (min USD 2)** at branches or agents.
- **External Account:** **2‰ (min USD 1)** at ATMs and **5‰ (min USD 2)** over the counter.

Fresh USD can also be wired abroad: **USD 25** from neo, or **USD 15 + 2‰ (min 30, max 500)** from the main Bank Audi app.

One caveat from 2023 commentary: dollars that arrive by **local** inter-bank transfer through BDL's Circular 165 clearing were reportedly *not* transferable to foreign correspondents. A wire received directly from abroad is a different flow. The monthly cash caps of about USD 400–800 reported in 2026 concern **legacy** deposits, not fresh accounts.

### Cited Findings
- **neo cash access:**
  - Cardless deposits and withdrawals at Bank Audi ATMs; withdrawals use an in-app **"Cash Out" voucher**.
  - Cash points also include **Cash United, OMT and Bob Finance**.
  - Sources: [App Store (Lebanon) listing](https://apps.apple.com/lb/app/neo-by-bank-audi/id6470289984); [neo home page](https://www.neo.com.lb/) **[official; via search summary]**
- **neo cash fees (Feb 2024 brochure):** Bank Audi ATM withdrawals **free**; branch and agent transactions **6‰, min USD 2 / LBP 100,000**. — [neo brochure (20 Feb 2024)](https://neofiles.blob.core.windows.net/files/Assets/ContentFiles/neoBROCHURE20Feb24_112218.pdf) **[official; 2024, may have changed]**
  - The USJ staff offer also lists free ATM withdrawals at Bank Audi ATMs, free official statements, and a **3% fee for loading money from other banks' cards**. — [USJ "neo offer" PDF (undated)](https://usj.edu.lb/ressourceshumaines/pdf/neo-offer.pdf) **[employer-distributed bank offer; may differ from general pricing]**
- **Bank Audi External Account (fresh):**
  - Monthly fee **USD 4** (Payroll or Personal External Account), **USD 5** (Business External Account).
  - Cash withdrawal **over the counter 5‰, min USD 2**; **ATM 2‰, min USD 1**.
  - Source: [Bank Audi, External Account](https://www.bankaudi.com.lb/externalaccount) **[official; via search summary]**
  - Bank Audi also publishes a page of **USD withdrawal locations for External Account holders**. — [Bank Audi, "USD Withdrawal Locations for External Account holders"](https://www.bankaudi.com.lb/usd-withdrawal-locations-for-external-account-holders) **[official; content not read]**
- **Transfers abroad:** neo outgoing international via IBAN **USD 25 / LBP 350,000**. — [neo, Packages](https://www.neo.com.lb/english/packages). Bank Audi app, External Account international transfer **USD 15 + 2‰ (min 30, max 500)**. — [Bank Audi, Mobile Application](https://www.bankaudi.com.lb/lebanon/adc/bank-audi-mobile-application) **[official]**
- **Circular 165 caveat (2023 commentary):**
  - Circular 165 "recreates a local clearing of fresh dollars within the BDL".
  - BDL "refuses to transfer the balances held in these clearing accounts to the banks' foreign correspondents until further notice".
  - The article assumed BDL would let banks withdraw those funds in cash.
  - Source: [L'Orient Today, "Circular 165 and the Prisoner's Dilemma" (2023)](https://today.lorientlejour.com/article/1341581/circular-165-and-the-prisoners-dilemma.html) **[secondary, opinion/analysis; may be outdated]**
- **2026 context:**
  - The banking sector "remains insolvent", depositors "remain subject to monthly cash withdrawal limits of roughly $400 to $800", and the branch network has shrunk to 633 branches.
  - These caps concern legacy deposits under BDL's withdrawal circulars. The same report says post-2019 deposits are treated separately and may generally be transferred abroad (see earlier notes).
  - Source: [US State Department, 2026 Investment Climate Statement – Lebanon (Sept 2026)](https://www.state.gov/reports/2026-investment-climate-statements/lebanon) **[official]**
- A draft **Financial Gap Law** (December 2025 draft) proposes scheduled repayments, and possibly a tax on some large pre-crisis transfers abroad unless the money is repatriated. — [Wikipedia, "Financial gap law (Lebanon)"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_gap_law_(Lebanon)) **[secondary, tertiary; status in earlier notes]**

### Inferences
- **Apple/Google wire from abroad vs Circular 165:** a SWIFT wire from Apple or Google reaches Bank Audi through its **foreign correspondent (nostro)**, not through BDL's local Circular 165 clearing. The funds should therefore be wire-able back out, consistent with State Department's "may generally be transferred abroad" for post-2019 money. The 2023 caveat applies mainly to dollars arriving by **local** transfer or cheque from another Lebanese bank. **This distinction is my inference.** Ask the bank to confirm in writing that incoming SWIFT funds on the account can be wired abroad.
- **Cheapest withdrawal path:** neo + Bank Audi ATM voucher, free per the 2024 brochure. On an External Account, the ATM costs 2‰ (USD 2 on USD 1,000).
- **Cheapest wire out:** neo's flat USD 25 is below the External Account's **minimum** of USD 30 (USD 15 + 2‰, min 30, max 500), so **neo is cheaper at every amount**, if neo's limits allow it. Example: USD 10,000 costs USD 25 from neo against USD 35 from the main app.
- Given the insolvency and resolution uncertainty in the earlier notes, keep only working balances in Lebanon and sweep surpluses out regularly. Either withdraw cash, or wire to a foreign account in the developer's own name if they have one.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 Bank Audi or BDL notice changing fresh-account rules was found. The State Department report (Sept 2026) is the latest general statement.
- Current neo cash fees (2026 version of the package table) were not confirmed. The 6‰ / free figures date from Feb 2024.
- Not confirmed: whether neo applies a daily or monthly **cash withdrawal** limit, and whether the "Cash Out" voucher has a per-voucher maximum.
- BDL's primary text of Circular 165 and Circular 150 was not read (bdl.gov.lb was not reached).

## 4. Bank Audi / neo cards: types, international e-commerce (Apple USD 99, Google USD 25, test subscriptions), 3-D Secure, foreign transaction fees

### Takeaway
neo gives **one physical and one instant digital debit card** in the Standard package. The card works "locally and abroad, online and in-store", and works internationally "in any foreign currency when linked to a fresh USD account". A **neo Cedar Miles credit card** also exists.

Fees:
- an international transaction fee of **1.5%** when the charge is in the account's currency, **4.8%** when it is not;
- the credit card's foreign-currency rate is also 4.8%.

3-D Secure with an SMS OTP is confirmed for the **teen** card. It is very likely the same for adult cards, but not confirmed.

Two anecdotal points matter to a developer:
- neo cards could **not be added to Apple Pay / Apple Wallet**, per a user review;
- the neo Android app **refuses to run with Developer Options enabled**.

### Inferences
- **Paying Apple's USD 99 and Google's USD 25:** a neo digital debit card linked to the **Fresh USD account** should work for both fees, which are online USD card charges with 3-D Secure.
  - Expected neo fee: **1.5%** of a same-currency charge, i.e. about **USD 1.49** on Apple's 99 and about **USD 0.38** on Google's 25.
  - The fee is **4.8%** if the card is set to another currency or the merchant bills in another currency.
  - Make sure the card's currency is switched to USD before paying.
  - These are my calculations from the package table.
- **Test subscriptions:** Apple sandbox and Google Play license-tester purchases are not charged to a card. Real test purchases on production (e.g. internal testing with real payment methods, or buying one's own subscription) need a card accepted by the user's **Apple ID / Google account** in its country storefront. Recurring small charges would each incur the 1.5% fee.
- **The Apple Pay gap** does not matter for these payments; a card entered directly works. **The Developer Options block matters a lot to an Android developer:** they will need to turn Developer Options off to use the neo app, or keep banking on a second phone.
- **Many Lebanese cards have had online or international usage switched off by default since 2019 (inference, not sourced here).** If a payment to Apple or Google is declined, first check the neo app's card settings (international / e-commerce toggles, currency) and the 3-D Secure OTP delivery to the registered mobile number.

## 5. Context: grey list / EU high-risk listing effects on wires into Lebanon; correspondent de-risking; Bank Audi's international network

### Takeaway
On top of the FATF and EU status in the earlier notes, the **US State Department (Sept 2026)** reports that international correspondent banks have **suspended credit lines** to Lebanese banks. That affects trade finance and credit, not the receipt of plain incoming payments. A secondary outlet reported **European banks severing correspondent relations** with Lebanon (date unverified). In October 2026 Lebanon revoked an Iranian bank's licence under US pressure.

No source documents Apple or Google payouts to Lebanon being rejected. The realistic effect is **extra screening and occasional delays** on EU-originated wires, for example from Apple Distribution International in Ireland. I found nothing usable on Bank Audi's current foreign subsidiaries.

### Cited Findings
- "International correspondent banks have suspended lines of credit to local institutions, forcing all trade and corporate investments to operate strictly through upfront cash funding or offshore financial structures." — [US State Department, 2026 Investment Climate Statement – Lebanon (Sept 2026)](https://www.state.gov/reports/2026-investment-climate-statements/lebanon); [PDF](https://www.state.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/701264_2026-Lebanon-Investment-Climate-Statement.pdf) **[official]**

The body was not read. — [The Cradle, "European banks begin severing correspondent relations with Lebanon: Report"](https://thecradle.co/article-view/25194/european-banks-begin-severing-correspondent-relations-with-lebanon-report) **[secondary; outlet with a strong editorial line; article date not seen, possibly 2024; relays another report]**
- Lebanon revoked an Iranian bank's licence amid US pressure over Hezbollah financing (October 2026). — [FDD's Long War Journal (Oct 2026)](https://www.longwarjournal.org/archives/2026/10/lebanon-revokes-iranian-banks-license-amid-us-pressure-over-hezbollah-financing.php); [FDD analysis](https://www.fdd.org/analysis/lebanon-revokes-iranian-banks-license-amid-us-pressure-over-hezbollah-financing/) **[secondary, think tank with a policy agenda]**

### Inferences
- The State Department's "suspended lines of credit" means Lebanese banks lack **credit** from correspondents, not that they cannot **receive** payments. Bank Audi still clearly receives USD wires: its published tariffs, neo's "receive international transfers" feature, and live third-party beneficiary sheets all show this.
- For an EU payer such as Apple Distribution International (Ireland), the EU high-risk listing means **enhanced due diligence at the payer's bank and the intermediaries**. Expect occasional requests for information that come back through Apple, and a few days' delay. Outright rejection is unlikely for a recurring, well-documented platform payout to a non-designated individual, though this is not sourced.
- A Bank Audi **subsidiary abroad** would only help if the developer can open an account there in their own name. It changes Apple's "bank country" and Google's eligibility rules (earlier notes, Q5). No current Bank Audi subsidiary was verified here.

- Not verified: Bank Audi's current international network in 2026 (Switzerland, France and others). Bank Audi's site and Wikipedia were not opened.
- The Cradle report's date and original source are not verified.
