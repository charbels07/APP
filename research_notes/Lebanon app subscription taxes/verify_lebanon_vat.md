# Verification of Lebanese VAT points: individual freelance app developer resident in Lebanon, selling app subscriptions worldwide through Apple and Google (as of 15 November 2026)

> **Method.** Read on 15 Nov 2026 directly from **finance.gov.lb** (MoF pages and PDFs), **legallaw.ul.edu.lb** (Lebanese University legal database) and **taxsummaries.pwc.com**. Arabic quotes are given with English translations. Status labels: **VERIFIED** = confirmed by an official text or PwC page actually read; **CORRECTED** = the earlier note (`lebanon_vat.md`) was wrong or imprecise; **STILL UNCONFIRMED** = not found in an official text that was read.
> **Source caveats.**
> - The legallaw consolidation of Law 379/2001 **lags**. It does not contain the Law 324/2024 text of Art. 3 (its Art. 3 page shows the 2017 version "in force until 12/02/2024") and contains none of the Law 40/2026 amendments.
> - The MoF's own VAT guidance pages are **outdated**: they still show a LBP 150 m threshold, a 20-day deadline and pre-2024 fines. For current rules, the primary texts used are the MoF PDFs of the budget laws.
> - Arabic text extracted from the Law 40/2026 PDF is partly garbled (font encoding). Law 324/2024 pages were rendered as images and read directly.
> - Two MoF PDFs listed on the MoF home page could not be downloaded (the server returned an empty reply): OL1531 and OL1657 (see §6).

## 1. Registration thresholds (mandatory / voluntary), Law 324/2024 Art. 14, Law 40/2026

### Takeaway
**VERIFIED:** mandatory registration applies above **LBP 5 billion over a period of one to four consecutive previous quarters**. **CORRECTED:** voluntary registration applies above **LBP 1 billion over a period of one to four consecutive previous quarters**, not "4 consecutive quarters". Both thresholds are in **Art. 3** of Law 379/2001 as replaced by **Art. 14 of Law 324/2024**. **VERIFIED (no change found):** Law 40/2026 does not amend Art. 3.

### Cited Findings
- **VERIFIED. Law 324/2024, Art. 14** ("تعديل نص المادة 3 من القانون رقم 379"), new **Art. 3(1)**: «يخضع للضريبة كل شخص طبيعي أو معنوي يقوم من خلال ممارسته نشاطا اقتصاديا بصورة مستقلة بعمليات تسليم أموال أو تقديم خدمات خاضعة للضريبة أو معفاة من الضريبة مع حق الحسم … شرط أن يتجاوز رقم الأعمال العائد لفترة تتراوح بين فصل وأربعة فصول متتالية سابقة مبلغ خمسة مليارات ليرة لبنانية». Translation: "Any natural or legal person who, while independently carrying on an economic activity, supplies goods or services that are taxable or exempt with right of deduction is subject to the tax, provided the turnover for a period of between one and four consecutive previous quarters exceeds LBP 5 billion." — [MoF PDF, Budget Law 2024, pp. 23–24](https://finance.gov.lb/ar-lb/Pages/Pdf-Preview.aspx?dt=OL1135) (law dated 12/2/2024; PDF created 21 Feb 2024).
- **VERIFIED (transition).** Same article: anyone whose turnover exceeded LBP 100 m but did not exceed LBP 5 bn in any period of 2020–2023 «يعفى … من موجب التسجيل في الضريبة على القيمة المضافة ومن كافة الموجبات الأخرى» ("is exempted … from the obligation to register for VAT and from all other obligations"). Persons registered on that basis «التقدم بطلب إلغاء تسجيلهم» ("may apply to deregister") — [same PDF, p. 23](https://finance.gov.lb/ar-lb/Pages/Pdf-Preview.aspx?dt=OL1135).
- **CORRECTED → VERIFIED. Voluntary threshold, Art. 3(2):** «يمكن لكل شخص يقوم بنشاط اقتصادي بصورة مستقلة خاضع للضريبة أو معفى من الضريبة مع حق الحسم … أن يطلب تسجيله اختياريا في الضريبة شرط أن يتجاوز رقم أعماله مبلغ مليار ليرة لبنانية خلال فترة تتراوح بين فصل وأربعة فصول متتالية سابقة». Translation: "… may request voluntary registration provided turnover exceeds LBP 1 billion during a period of between one and four consecutive previous quarters." — [same PDF, p. 24](https://finance.gov.lb/ar-lb/Pages/Pdf-Preview.aspx?dt=OL1135).
- **VERIFIED.** The MoF Citizen Budget 2024 summary: «المادة 14 – الضريبة على القيمة المضافة (5 مليار بدلاً من 100 مليون)» ("Art. 14 – VAT (5 billion instead of 100 million)") — [MoF, Citizen Budget 2024 (PDF created 8 Apr 2024)](https://finance.gov.lb/en-us/Pages/Pdf-Preview.aspx?dt=OL1138).
- **VERIFIED. Exports count toward turnover.** Law 379/2001 **Art. 4** includes «قيمة العمليات المنصوص عليها في المواد 19 و 20 و 21» ("the value of the operations in Arts 19, 20 and 21": exports, international transport, certain agents' services) and excludes the VAT itself — [legallaw, Law 379/2001 Arts 2–15](https://legallaw.ul.edu.lb/LawArticles.aspx?LawTreeSectionID=269924&LawID=203155&language=ar) (law of 14/12/2001, Official Gazette 24/12/2001). This confirms the earlier inference.
- **VERIFIED (negative). Law 40/2026.** Its articles that amend Law 379 are Art. 30 (amends Art. 28), Art. 31 (Art. 30), Art. 32 (Art. 59), Art. 41 (extra time for 2024 Art. 59 refunds) and Art. 51 (Arts 35(4) and 39). **None amends Art. 3 or Art. 25** — [MoF PDF, Budget Law 2026 approved by Parliament (published on the MoF site 13 Feb 2026)](https://finance.gov.lb/ar-lb/Pages/Pdf-Preview.aspx?dt=OL1504). The 2025 article "budget proposal raises the threshold" (earlier note) is therefore not reflected in the enacted law.
- **VERIFIED, with an internal inconsistency in PwC.** "Any taxable person who achieves a total turnover in excess of LL 5 billion over a period of one to four consecutive quarters must register for VAT." — [PwC Individual – Other taxes (last reviewed 01 July 2026)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/lebanon/individual/other-taxes). The same page still carries an outdated line ("turnover that exceeds LL 150 million over four successive quarters"). The Corporate page also keeps an outdated LL 100 m line before stating "The 2024 Budget Law increased the threshold … to LL 5 billion over a period of one to four consecutive quarters" — [PwC Corporate – Other taxes (01 July 2026)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/lebanon/corporate/other-taxes). PwC does not mention the LBP 1 bn voluntary threshold.
- **CORRECTED (date).** Law 324 is **dated 12/2/2024**: Law 40/2026 Art. 52 cites «القانون رقم 324 تاريخ 2024/2/12» and gives 15/2/2024 as the 2024 budget's effective date — [Law 40/2026 PDF](https://finance.gov.lb/ar-lb/Pages/Pdf-Preview.aspx?dt=OL1504). PwC says it was published in Official Gazette No. 7 on 15 Feb 2024.
- **Outdated official page.** The MoF registration page still reads «رقم الأعمال يتجاوز الحد الأدنى للخضوع (حالياً 150 مليون ل.ل.)» ("turnover exceeds the minimum (currently LBP 150 m)") and cites Art. 3 as amended in 2004 — [MoF, التسجيل في الضريبة (undated; read 15 Nov 2026)](https://www.finance.gov.lb/ar-lb/Taxation/Companies/VAT/Pages/Registration-in-VAT.aspx).
- **Registration deadline.** Art. 35(1): «طلب تسجيل … في مهلة شهرين إبتداء من آخر يوم من الفصل الذي تكون قد توفرت خلاله شروط الخضوع» ("registration application within two months from the last day of the quarter in which the conditions were met") — [legallaw, Arts 33–41](https://legallaw.ul.edu.lb/LawArticles.aspx?LawTreeSectionID=269934&LawID=203155&language=ar).

### Inferences
- Law 324/2024 Art. 14 sets the thresholds as fixed LBP amounts. It does not let the MoF index them.
- "Turnover" is the value of the operations excluding VAT. The law does not say whether, for App Store or Google Play income, this means the consumer price or the net proceeds paid to the developer. That depends on who the developer's customer is (see §3).

### Gaps
- The Official Gazette text itself was not read. The MoF PDF of Law 324/2024 is the official MoF publication.

## 2. Must exporters register regardless of turnover? Does this cover exporters of services?

### Takeaway
**VERIFIED (statute):** «المصدر والمستورد» (the exporter and the importer) must register **whatever their turnover**: Art. 3(4) as replaced by Law 324/2024. **CORRECTED:** the registration article is **Art. 3**, not Art. 32/33. **VERIFIED (official MoF interpretation, 2017):** the MoF read the identical earlier wording as covering **exports or imports of goods or services**. **STILL UNCONFIRMED:** no post-2024 MoF text restates this, and none applies it to App Store or Google Play income.

### Cited Findings
- **VERIFIED. Law 324/2024 Art. 14, new Art. 3(4):** «يخضع للضريبة المصدر والمستورد الذي يقوم من خلال ممارسته نشاطا اقتصاديا بصورة مستقلة بعمليات تسليم أموال أو تقديم خدمات خاضعة للضريبة أو معفاة من الضريبة مع حق الحسم وفقا لأحكام هذا القانون، مهما بلغ رقم أعماله». Translation: "The exporter and the importer who, while independently carrying on an economic activity, supplies goods **or provides services** that are taxable or exempt with right of deduction are subject to the tax, **whatever their turnover**." — [MoF PDF, Budget Law 2024, p. 24](https://finance.gov.lb/ar-lb/Pages/Pdf-Preview.aspx?dt=OL1135). The text does not limit "exporter" to goods.
- **VERIFIED (history).** The same rule was Art. 3(2) as amended by Law 66/2017 (in force 03/11/2017–12/02/2024), worded identically — [legallaw, Art. 3 version history](https://legallaw.ul.edu.lb/LawArticles.aspx?LawArticleID=1083464&LawID=203155).
- **VERIFIED. MoF interpretive notice (إعلام), 19/12/2017, "تحديد رقم الأعمال الإلزامي والإختياري للتسجيل":** registration conditions are met «عند القيام ابتداءً من 8/11/2017 بعمليات تصدير أو استيراد لأموال أو خدمات مهما بلغ رقم الأعمال». Translation: "when carrying out, from 8/11/2017, export or import operations **of goods or services**, whatever the turnover." Such persons must apply «خلال مهلة شهرين من قيامهم بأول عملية تصدير أو استيراد» ("within two months of their first export or import") — [legallaw, MoF notice 19/12/2017](https://legallaw.ul.edu.lb/Law.aspx?lawId=274856); also listed on the MoF site as [OL181](https://finance.gov.lb/ar-lb/Pages/Pdf-Preview.aspx?dt=OL181).
- **VERIFIED. Obligations reach zero-rated suppliers.** Art. 34: the obligations of Book 9 apply to «ب - الأشخاص الذين يقومون بعمليات معفاة من الضريبة تتيح لهم حق الحسم وفقا للبند (2) من الفقرة الاولى من المادة 28» ("persons who carry out exempt operations giving a right of deduction under Art. 28(1)(2)") — [legallaw, Arts 33–41](https://legallaw.ul.edu.lb/LawArticles.aspx?LawTreeSectionID=269934&LawID=203155&language=ar).
- **VERIFIED. MoF on offshore companies:** they become taxable on «القيام بنشاط اقتصادي خاضع للضريبة أو معفى مع حق الحسم (مثلا تصدير بضاعة أو خدمات من لبنان إلى الخارج…)» ("taxable or exempt-with-deduction activity, e.g. export of goods **or services** from Lebanon abroad"). That condition also requires the threshold and four quarters, so the page is pre-2017 in substance — [MoF registration page (read 15 Nov 2026)](https://www.finance.gov.lb/ar-lb/Taxation/Companies/VAT/Pages/Registration-in-VAT.aspx).
- **VERIFIED.** "Importers and exporters should register for VAT irrespective of their turnover." — [PwC Individual – Other taxes (01 July 2026)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/lebanon/individual/other-taxes). PwC does not say whether this covers services.
- **VERIFIED (enforcement climate). MoF notice 4/ص1 (early January 2026; date partly illegible "2 … 2026"; it refers to the Q4-2025 deadline of 20/1/2026), signed by Minister Yassine Jaber.** It reminds taxpayers that tax evasion includes «عدم التسجيل» ("non-registration"). Unregistered persons who meet the conditions must register and file the quarterly returns for the late periods. The VAT directorate «سوف تعمل فور انتهاء تلك المهلة، إلى ملاحقة المكلفين المتخلفين عن التسجيل … وإلى استدراك الضرائب والغرامات المتوجبة عليهم وإحالتهم إلى النيابة العامة المالية» ("will, once that deadline passes, pursue taxpayers who failed to register … recover the taxes and fines due and refer them to the Financial Public Prosecutor") — [MoF, إعلام موجه الى المكلفين بالضريبة على القيمة المضافة](https://finance.gov.lb/ar-lb/Pages/Pdf-Preview.aspx?dt=OL1460).

### Inferences
- On the statute and the MoF's only official reading (2017), a Lebanese resident whose sales are **exports of services** is an "exporter" who must register **from the first export, whatever the turnover**. The application is due within two months (2017 notice), or by Art. 35(1) timing.
- This reverses the earlier note's guess that "exporters" probably means customs (goods) operators. The conservative reading is that the developer must register.
- Law 324/2024 re-enacted the identical wording, so the 2017 reading most likely still applies. No post-2024 notice confirms this.

### Gaps
- No MoF notice after 2024 on Art. 3(4) was found.
- No MoF ruling was found on whether an App Store / Google Play developer is an "exporter". A written query to the VAT directorate is still advisable.

## 3. Export of services: legal basis, conditions, right of deduction, MoF circulars

### Takeaway
**VERIFIED:** **Art. 19(1)** of Law 379/2001 exempts «تقديم الخدمات المستعملة خارج الأراضي اللبنانية» (services used outside Lebanon). The right of deduction comes from **Art. 28(1)(2)** (re-enacted by Law 40/2026 Art. 30) and **Decree 7284/2002**. **CORRECTED:**
- The test is **use outside Lebanon**, and the person claiming it must **prove** that use.
- **Decree 7309/2002 Art. 6** presumes that a service is used **in Lebanon** whenever either party to the contract is established or resides in Lebanon, until the contrary is proven.
- The statute contains no separate "non-resident recipient" condition.
- "Art. 30" is not the export article: it governs excess credit and refunds.

No MoF circular on apps, platforms or freelancers was found (**STILL UNCONFIRMED / none found**).

### Cited Findings
- **VERIFIED. Art. 2:** taxable operations are supplies «التي تتم داخل الأراضي اللبنانية من قبل شخص خاضع للضريبة» ("made within Lebanese territory by a taxable person"). **Art. 14 (place of supply of services):** «يعتبر تقديم الخدمات حاصلا في لبنان إذا إستعملت الخدمة داخل الأراضي اللبنانية» ("a supply of services takes place in Lebanon if the service is used within Lebanese territory"). Exceptions apply for services relating to real estate and to movable goods — [legallaw, Arts 2–15](https://legallaw.ul.edu.lb/LawArticles.aspx?LawTreeSectionID=269924&LawID=203155&language=ar).
- **VERIFIED. Art. 19 (amended), title «إعفاء التصدير والأعمال المشابهة»:** «تعفى من الضريبة الأعمال التالية: 1 - تسليم اموال مرسلة او منقولة الى خارج الأراضي اللبنانية, وتقديم الخدمات المستعملة خارج الأراضي اللبنانية». Translation: "The following are exempt: 1 – supplies of goods sent or transported outside Lebanese territory, **and supplies of services used outside Lebanese territory**." — [legallaw, Arts 19–21](https://legallaw.ul.edu.lb/LawArticles.aspx?LawTreeSectionID=269928&LawID=203155&language=ar).
- **VERIFIED. Art. 21:** services of agents acting «باسم ولحساب موكليهم» ("in the name and for the account of their principals") are exempt when they relate to Art. 19/20 operations «أو عمليات حاصلة خارج الأراضي اللبنانية» ("or to operations taking place outside Lebanese territory"). **Art. 12:** a taxable agent acting «بإسمه الشخصي ولحساب موكله» ("in his own name for the account of his principal") is treated as having made the supply himself — [legallaw](https://legallaw.ul.edu.lb/LawArticles.aspx?LawTreeSectionID=269924&LawID=203155&language=ar).
- **VERIFIED. Decree 7284 of 25/01/2002 (implementing Arts 19–21).**
  - Art. 3(ث): the exemption covers «عمليات تقديم الخدمات التي يثبت انها مستعملة خارج الاراضي اللبنانية» ("services **proven** to be used outside Lebanese territory").
  - Art. 2: the right to deduct input VAT is granted «شرط ان تتوفر لدى هذا الشخص شروط الخضوع للضريبة» ("provided the person meets the conditions for being subject to the tax"). A Minister's decision sets the supporting documents to keep.
  - [legallaw, Decree 7284/2002](https://legallaw.ul.edu.lb/LawArticles.aspx?LawTreeSectionID=199553&LawID=197314&language=ar)
- **VERIFIED (key presumption). Decree 7309 of 28/01/2002, Art. 6:** «تعتبر الخدمة مستعملة داخل الاراضي اللبنانية ولحين ثبوت العكس, بمجرد اقامة أحد فريقي العقد في لبنان, كأن يكون لاحدهما محل استثماري في لبنان … او محل سكن معتاد في لبنان». Translation: "A service is deemed used within Lebanon, **until the contrary is proven**, as soon as **one of the parties to the contract** resides in Lebanon, e.g. has a business establishment … or habitual residence there." — [legallaw, Decree 7309/2002](https://legallaw.ul.edu.lb/LawArticles.aspx?LawTreeSectionID=199538&LawID=197299&language=ar).
- **VERIFIED. MoF (Levying the VAT):** «العمليات المعفاة مع حق الحسم هي العمليات الخاضعة للضريبة بمعدل صفر%» ("exempt-with-deduction operations are operations taxed at 0%"). Examples given: export and international transport — [MoF, Levying the VAT (undated; read 15 Nov 2026)](https://www.finance.gov.lb/ar-lb/Taxation/Individuals/VAT/Pages/Levying-the-VAT.aspx).
- **VERIFIED. Deduction, Art. 28 as re-enacted by Law 40/2026 Art. 30:** deductible VAT covers inputs used for «العمليات المتعلقة بالتصدير والعمليات المشابهة … المعفاة من الضريبة بموجب المواد 19 و 20 و 21» ("operations relating to export and similar operations … exempt under Arts 19, 20 and 21"). **New from 2026:** VAT on «استهلاك الماء والكهرباء والاتصالات والإنترنت والبنزين» ("water, electricity, telecommunications, **internet** and petrol") is **not deductible**. Cars are capped at a USD 30,000 value — [Law 40/2026 PDF, Art. 30, pp. ~39–40](https://finance.gov.lb/ar-lb/Pages/Pdf-Preview.aspx?dt=OL1504). PwC states the same ("Utility … telecommunications, Internet, and fuel expenses") — [PwC Individual – Other taxes (01 July 2026)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/lebanon/individual/other-taxes).
- **VERIFIED, with a CORRECTION to PwC's wording. Refunds, Art. 30 as replaced by Law 40/2026 Art. 31.**
  - Excess credit carries forward.
  - An annual refund request can be made within one month after year-end.
  - «يحق للمصدرين أن يقدموا، بعد نهاية أية فترة احتساب للضريبة وضمن مهلة شهر، طلب استرداد رصيد فائض الضريبة القابلة للحسم … على أن لا يقل المبلغ المطالب باسترداده عن مائتي مليون ليرة لبنانية» ("**exporters** may, after the end of any tax period and within one month, request a refund of the excess deductible tax … provided the amount claimed is **not less than LBP 200 million**").
  - The administration decides within three months.
  - [Law 40/2026 PDF, Art. 31, pp. ~40–41](https://finance.gov.lb/ar-lb/Pages/Pdf-Preview.aspx?dt=OL1504)
  - PwC calls this a "LL 200 million cap" ([PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/lebanon/individual/other-taxes)). In law it is a **minimum** (floor), not a cap.
- **VERIFIED, slightly CORRECTED.** PwC: "Export of goods and services and export-related services, international transport, and some of the intermediate operations are zero-rated." It also says: "Note that the recharge of expenses from an entity in Lebanon to another entity abroad is subject to VAT at 11%." — [PwC Corporate – Other taxes (01 July 2026)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/lebanon/corporate/other-taxes). The current page does **not** say "since 2013", contrary to the earlier note.
- **STILL UNCONFIRMED / none found.** No MoF circular, notice or decision on exported services, freelancers with foreign clients, online platforms, the App Store or Google Play was found:
  - none in the MoF home-page document list read on 15 Nov 2026 (VAT items 2017–2026);
  - none on the VAT pages read;
  - none among the decrees and decisions linked to Arts 19–21 on legallaw.

### Inferences
- **Proof is on the developer.** Under Decree 7309 Art. 6, the developer is a party resident in Lebanon, so its services are presumed used in Lebanon until it proves otherwise.
- **If the customer is Apple or Google** (non-resident platforms acting in their own name), the developer can show that the service (the app licence and the content Apple or Google resell) is used outside Lebanon. Evidence: the Apple/Google agreements, financial reports by country, and payments from abroad.
- **If the customers are the end users**, subscribers in Lebanon fail the "used outside Lebanon" test. Under Art. 12, an agent acting in its own name is treated as the supplier, but that rule is written for **taxable** (Lebanese-registered) agents. How it applies to foreign platforms is unaddressed.
- A **registered** exporter who is mostly zero-rated will rarely reach the LBP 200 m minimum refund. Since 2026, internet and telecom input VAT is non-deductible anyway.

### Gaps
- The Minister's decision listing proof documents for exports (Decree 7284 Art. 2) was not located.

## 4. Reverse charge (Art. 40) and 2026 plans to tax foreign digital services or apps

### Takeaway
**VERIFIED:** under **Art. 40(2)**, a Lebanese resident who uses in Lebanon a service acquired from abroad must declare and pay the VAT **whatever its value**. A registered person pays in the periodic return. **CORRECTED (earlier gap resolved):** a **non-registered** beneficiary pays through a **special return** (Decree 7309 Art. 10). **STILL UNCONFIRMED:** no official MoF text was found on the September 2026 ministerial statement about taxing foreign digital services or apps.

### Cited Findings
- **VERIFIED. Art. 40(2):** «على كل شخص مقيم في لبنان يستعمل فيه خدمة إكتسبها من جهة مقيمة خارج الأراضي اللبنانية, أن يصرح على الضريبة المتوجبة عن هذه الخدمة مهما بلغت قيمتها ويؤديها الى الإدارة الضريبية». Translation: "Every person resident in Lebanon who uses there a service acquired from a party resident abroad must declare the VAT due on it, whatever its value, and pay it to the tax administration." **Art. 33(1)** makes that resident the person liable — [legallaw, Arts 33–41](https://legallaw.ul.edu.lb/LawArticles.aspx?LawTreeSectionID=269934&LawID=203155&language=ar).
- **VERIFIED. Decree 7309/2002 Art. 10:** the beneficiary pays «ضمن تصريحه الدوري اذا كان خاضعا للضريبة, وبموجب تصريح خاص … في حال لم يكن المستفيد من الخدمة خاضعا للضريبة» ("in its periodic return if taxable, and by a special return … if the beneficiary is not taxable") — [legallaw, Decree 7309/2002](https://legallaw.ul.edu.lb/LawArticles.aspx?LawTreeSectionID=199538&LawID=197299&language=ar).
- **VERIFIED. Decree 7837/2002 Art. 2:** «لا يتوجب تعيين ممثل بالنسبة للخدمات التي تؤدى من الخارج والتي يستفيد منها شخص مقيم في لبنان» ("no representative need be appointed for services performed from abroad that benefit a person resident in Lebanon") — [legallaw, Decree 7837/2002](https://legallaw.ul.edu.lb/LawArticles.aspx?LawTreeSectionID=182388&LawID=180149&language=ar). The MoF Declaration page says the same — [MoF, التصريح عن الضريبة (read 15 Nov 2026)](https://www.finance.gov.lb/ar-lb/Taxation/Individuals/VAT/Pages/Declaration.aspx).
- **STILL UNCONFIRMED. Ministerial statement of ~20 Sep 2026** on taxing foreign digital services (streaming, cloud, paid apps, online advertising). Official sources show nothing:
  - No MoF news item or document on it appears in the MoF home-page lists read on 15 Nov 2026.
  - In the MoF PDF of the **2027 draft budget** sent to Parliament ([OL1644](https://finance.gov.lb/ar-lb/Pages/Pdf-Preview.aspx?dt=OL1644)), the extracted article headings (78 articles) show **no article amending the VAT law** and none on digital services. The only VAT entries found were revenue lines. Text extraction is partly garbled, so this is not conclusive.

### Inferences
- In law, Art. 40(2) already reaches **consumers** (non-registered residents) who buy foreign digital services. No sign of enforcement against consumers was found.
- For the developer, once registered, Art. 40(2) means self-accounting 11% on foreign services used in Lebanon: the Apple Developer fee, cloud hosting, SaaS, and possibly the platform commission under an agency reading. It is normally deductible in the same return, except categories made non-deductible since 2026 (internet and telecom).

### Gaps
- No official text, draft article or MoF statement was found on a foreign-platform VAT regime.

## 5. VAT rate: 11% and the proposed 12%

### Takeaway

## 6. Return frequency and deadline; penalties (late registration, late filing, late payment)

### Takeaway
- **VERIFIED:** returns are **quarterly** (calendar quarter).
- **VERIFIED:** since **Law 40/2026 Art. 51**, the return **and payment** are due **within one month** after the quarter, instead of 20 days.
- **CORRECTED, late filing:** **10% of the tax due per month**, capped at 100%, minimum **LBP 2.5 m for individuals** from 2026 business. Sources: Law 324/2024 Art. 74, then Law 40/2026 Art. 17. The 5% rate and LBP 100,000 minimum on the MoF fines page are outdated.
- **CORRECTED, late payment:** **3% per month** for VAT (Law 324/2024 Art. 77).
- **Registration fine:** lump-sum fines in Law 44/2008 Arts 107, 108, 111 and others were **raised ×25** from 2026 business (Law 40/2026 Art. 16). The current LBP amount of the failure-to-register fine is **STILL UNCONFIRMED**.

### Inferences
- For Q4 2026 (ending 31 Dec 2026), the return and payment fall due about one month later (≈ 31 Jan 2027), subject to MoF extensions and to how notice OL1531 counts the month.
- If Art. 107 is the registration fine and it was not multiplied before 2026, an individual's fine for late registration from 2026 would be LBP 300,000 × 25 = **LBP 7.5 m**. This is **not verified** (see Gaps).
- For a pure exporter the tax-based penalties (10%/month, 3%/month) apply to little or no tax. The **fixed minimum of LBP 2.5 m per late return** still applies, if the minimum applies to nil returns.
